# AnkiNotch v0.1.0-beta.1

The first public beta: review Anki cards in a panel that expands from your Mac's notch or the top center of another display. Anki remains the scheduler.

## Included

- Hover to open; move away to collapse.
- Space to reveal, Space for Good, and `1` for Again.
- Deck selection by clicking the notch, plus ⌘Z undo for the current session's answers.
- Card HTML/CSS/JavaScript and local images.
- Bouncy expansion and collapse, enabled by default with a Settings toggle. Respects Reduce Motion.
- Optional forced black background.

## Install

Requires **macOS 15+**, **Anki Desktop**, and **AnkiConnect** (add-on code `2055492159`) running locally at its default endpoint. Download and unzip **AnkiNotch-v0.1.0-beta.1-universal.zip**, move **AnkiNotch.app** to Applications, and open it. See the [setup guide](https://github.com/script-amv/anki-notch#install-and-connect).

The download includes Apple silicon and Intel builds. Apple silicon has been tested; the Intel binary is cross-compiled and has not been tested on hardware.

**This beta is ad-hoc signed and not notarized.** If macOS blocks opening it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway after attempting to open it. See [Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

## Limitations

Only Again and Good are available. No audio/TTS, automatic updates, or automatic launch at login. Custom card templates may render differently. A camera notch can obscure content at the top center. Settings and Quit are available from the menu-bar icon.

Reviews change your real Anki collection. Back it up and avoid reviewing in both apps simultaneously. The app has no analytics; card scripts and external resources may still access the network.

Free and open source under MIT. Developed with AI coding assistance. Please [report reproducible problems](https://github.com/script-amv/anki-notch/issues) with your macOS/Anki versions and Mac chip, using sample or redacted cards.
